About Me

Who am I?

I'm a passionate IT Specialist based in Lusaka with 8 years of experience. I build responsive web apps and distinctive brand identities, craft photoreal 3D visuals, and deliver CCTV, access control, networking and digital marketing solutions that keep Zambian businesses secure and visible.

My work includes the Society260 community platform, GraciMor property and payroll systems, and ecommerce builds for My Perfect Stitch and The Beadroom. I also produce brand identity collections, company profiles, brochures, posters and photoreal 3D product scenes.

I also handle Starlink and office network setups, Hikvision CCTV and attendance systems, and TikTok or Facebook growth campaigns for local brands. Whether the brief is a new website, a security upgrade or a campaign, I focus on solutions that are practical, secure and built to last.
